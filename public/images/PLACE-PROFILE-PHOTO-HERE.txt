PROFILE PHOTO — how to add it
==============================

1. Save your professional profile photo in THIS folder (public/images/).

2. Name it exactly:
       aakshat-profile.webp

   Full path:
       public/images/aakshat-profile.webp

3. Recommended preparation:
       • Format:      WebP (preferred) — PNG/JPG also work if you rename to .webp
       • Orientation: Portrait
       • Size:        at least 840 x 1050 px (min ~1200px tall is ideal)
       • Background:  clean or lightly blurred; a transparent cut-out also works
       • Crop:        head-and-shoulders, face clearly visible and roughly centred

4. That's it — the site auto-detects the file. Until you add it, the portrait
   shows a clean "AA" monogram fallback instead of a broken image.

To use a different filename or path, change `profileImage` in:
       data/portfolio.ts

To fine-tune the crop, adjust the `objectPosition` prop where <ProfilePortrait>
is used (in components/sections/Hero.tsx and components/sections/About.tsx).

You can delete this .txt file once the photo is in place.
